Reading notes

  1. Realtime Database has no joins. Lists such as orders.lines, payments, favorites and addOnPrefs are embedded maps on their parent node.
  2. orders/{id} → sales/{id} is a one-way, denormalised copy written when an order settles (or is closed as Unclaimed), so reports scan sales bounded by the indexed soldAt.
  3. proofs/{id} holds the screenshot only; the order or booking stays lean. Proofs are excluded from backups and are deleted seven days after a terminal state.
  4. staff is keyed by the Google sub and is checked on every request by the staff.role middleware; users is the customer identity keyed by the same sub.
  5. Security rules require auth != null for all reads and writes, and every queryable tree carries the .indexOn fields used by the bounded queries.
